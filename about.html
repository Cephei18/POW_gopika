I’m Gopika, a computer science student at IIT Mandi building at the intersection of AI systems, Ethereum infrastructure, and internet coordination.

My work usually lives somewhere between backend engineering and experimental internet architecture: autonomous ecosystem intelligence platforms, Farcaster-native social systems, DeFi tooling, protocol mechanics, and AI agents that probably sound slightly absurd until they start working in production.

I’m especially interested in systems that operate at scale quietly: asynchronous pipelines, real-time data flows, protocol integrations, caching layers, deterministic scoring systems, and infrastructure that turns chaotic information into something useful.

A lot of my projects begin with a niche technical curiosity:

“Can this be automated?”
“Can coordination feel smarter?”
“Can the internet behave more like an adaptive system?”

Then I disappear into terminal windows, architecture diagrams, Redis caches, and far too many open tabs until the idea becomes real.

Outside engineering, I spend a lot of time building developer communities, mentoring students, organizing workshops, and helping more people enter technical spaces that once felt inaccessible to them.

I take my work seriously. I just don’t believe serious people have to be boring.
